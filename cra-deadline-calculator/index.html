How these deadlines are calculated

  • They are outer limits. Article 14 requires filing without undue delay, and in any event within 24 and 72 hours.
  • They use the strictest reading: exact hours from the triggering moment, and calendar days and months with no rounding and no weekend extension. Where a time zone or a daylight-saving change could move a date, the earlier result is shown. Filing by these times is on time.
  • The ENISA platform's own 72-hour counter shows a due date 48 hours after your early warning is submitted, not 72 hours after you became aware. Keep your own clock.
  • The coordinating CSIRT may also request an intermediate report at any time, with a deadline it sets (Article 14(6)).
